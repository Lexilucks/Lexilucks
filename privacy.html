Privacy Policy

Effective April 28, 2026. This page explains how lexilucks.com collects and uses information for the Lexi Lucks creator business.

Information Collected

Forms may collect name, email, phone number, Instagram handle, inquiry type, consent choices, referrer, landing page, and UTM campaign fields. The AI Concierge stores anonymous browser-local preferences so it can remember which site lane a visitor seems interested in.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used for Party Network updates, email/SMS reminders where consent is given, membership interest, brand collaboration inquiries, live-stream/community updates, and Transcend/community routing. Creator partnership inquiries and community/nonprofit inquiries are routed separately.

Social Platform Integrations (Meta / Instagram / TikTok)

This site and its associated creator tools (LexiOS) may integrate with Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and TikTok APIs to publish content, read page/account metrics, and manage posts on behalf of the page owner (Lexi Lucks). These integrations are used exclusively for the Lexi Lucks creator business. Data obtained through Meta or TikTok APIs is used only to operate and improve these creator tools and is not shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising targeting. All API access tokens are stored securely server-side and never exposed to end users or third parties. Use of data obtained through Meta APIs complies with the Meta Platform Terms and Meta Developer Policies. Use of TikTok API data complies with TikTok's Developer Terms of Service.

Analytics

The site includes a lightweight event layer that can connect to analytics providers such as Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, or PostHog. Until those providers are connected, events are logged locally for testing.

SMS And Email

SMS messages are only intended for users who opt in. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out when SMS is connected. Email recipients can unsubscribe through the provider once email automation is connected.

Data Retention And Deletion

You may request deletion of any personal data collected through this site or its associated creator tools by contacting hello@lexilucks.com. Requests will be processed within 30 days.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@lexilucks.com.
